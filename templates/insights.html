How Predictions Work

RiskRadar uses a weighted scoring model to assess academic risk based on seven key factors: midterm scores, assignments, quizzes, participation, projects, attendance, and study hours.

Each factor is weighted according to its correlation with overall academic success. The resulting score determines the risk band:

High Score below 60 — immediate intervention recommended
Moderate Score 60–69 — monitor closely, suggest improvements
Low Score 70+ — on track, encourage continued effort
